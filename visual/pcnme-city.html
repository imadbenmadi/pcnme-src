The map is the VFC grid of the thesis (1200 m × 1200 m, roads every 200 m, zones of radius 350 m). Every time, size and cost in the panels comes from the design and the config; the Q-values are example values.

Cars are never MQTT clients. Task inputs never travel over MQTT. No decision compares clocks of two machines: messages carry budgets, durations and versions.

Numbers used: T1 frame S = 76 KB = 608 kbit, result 3 KB, D = 100 ms (hard); T4 D = 1000 ms (soft, Lmax = D); T2 clip 4572 KB. Radio 40 MHz at 5.9 GHz (Shannon), backhaul 1 Gbit/s with delay 1–5 ms (mean 3 ms), WAN 36 Mbit/s with delay 25–100 ms (mean 62.5 ms), broker +1 ms. Fog GTX 1070 time table, cloud A100.
