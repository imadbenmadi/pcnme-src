### Findings

Per scenario: PCNME's on-time share, the best method's, and the paired tests (on time, p < 0.05) that PCNME wins, ties or loses; refusal rates from report.csv, predictor accuracy from extra-runs.csv.

- **S1.** PCNME 99.9 % on time; best Greedy 99.9 %. Light load: every method is at its ceiling; the differences are hundredths of a percent.
  Wins: VFC PPO (+0.10 pp, p < 0.001), Tang & Wong (+0.09 pp, p < 0.001), QECO (+0.08 pp, p < 0.001), Random (+0.35 pp, p < 0.001). Ties: D3QN (scratch) (+0.01 pp, p 0.192). Loses: Greedy (-0.02 pp, p < 0.001), D3QN + greedy demos (-0.02 pp, p 0.018).
- **S2.** PCNME 99.8 % on time; best PCNME 99.8 %. Medium load: PCNME, Greedy and D3QN + greedy demos are at the ceiling; the methods without demonstrations lose a little.
  Wins: D3QN (scratch) (+0.17 pp, p < 0.001), VFC PPO (+0.67 pp, p < 0.001), Tang & Wong (+1.21 pp, p < 0.001), QECO (+1.15 pp, p < 0.001), Random (+1.66 pp, p < 0.001). Ties: Greedy (+0.02 pp, p 0.411), D3QN + greedy demos (+0.01 pp, p 0.093). Loses: none.
- **S3.** PCNME 98.4 % on time; best PCNME 98.4 %. Overload (rho 1.5): PCNME's placements are refused by a full node less often than Greedy's (refusal rate 0.6 vs 1.3 %), and it keeps the most tasks on time.

## Limitations

- No ablations: the 16.4 experiments (expert data, FedAvg variants, queue order, refusal penalty, IQL, 60 inputs, state period, sensitivity sweeps) and the training-budget test are future work.
- One training budget of 1 M decisions per method and scenario. Several learners were still improving when it ran out: on LuST most of them in S3, S4 and S6, on the grid the D3QN family in S3 (the Training tables: the change over the last 20 episodes). Each episode is another seed, so a change of a few tenths is within episode-to-episode noise.
- The testbed runs at 40 cars (2 car containers) and 60 s per run, on one PC; the fast-mode evaluation runs 200 cars. Under overload (S3) many results reach the cars after they gave the task up, so these tasks count as failed where fast mode drops them (investigated for Greedy, RUN_LOG 15 Nov); the high failed shares of the LSTM baselines in S4 and S5 are not analysed.
- FedAvg + fine-tuning (13.7, phase 2) is evaluated in fast mode only; on the testbed it is future work (QUESTIONS Q64).
- LuST has no testbed: its results are fast mode only.
- The 16.3 metrics without a definition in the design use stand-ins (QUESTIONS Q63); delivery after a zone change has few tasks per run and is not reported.
- S6 (mixed fog) is not analysed beyond its tables (PLAN T6).
