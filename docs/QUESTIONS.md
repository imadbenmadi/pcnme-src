| Q7 | 2026-10-28 | M1.4 (blocks only the default shape; M1.5–M1.8 and the M1 acceptance tests do not depend on it) | What distribution do run times follow? A time-table row gives only mean, p5 and p95 (14.4), and a run time is u ~ (0, 1) mapped through an inverse CDF (C3), so a shape is needed. With the placeholder factors (p5 = 0.9 ×, p95 = 1.3 × the mean) no lognormal fits all three numbers: p5 · p95 = median² forces median = 1.082 × mean, but a lognormal's mean exceeds its median. No shifted lognormal fits either: over every shift, the mean of the one matching p5 and p95 is at least 1.002 × the target (the closest misses by 0.2 %). Options include a shifted lognormal fitted to p5 and p95 (mean 0.2 % high), a piecewise-linear CDF through (min, p5, mean-fixing knot, p95, max), or a two-parameter fit to mean and p95 that leaves p5 free. The code keeps the shape a separate, swappable piece behind one interface (inverse CDF of u); the tests use exponential and deterministic shapes only, and no default is set. | 28 Oct, Imed: a lognormal fitted to the mean and p95, with the placeholder p5 factor changed from 0.9 to 0.75 (the p5 of that lognormal: σ = 0.168, p5 = 0.748 × the mean). The mean (Ê, B, R, ρ_fog, E_ref) and p95 (Ê^high, the watchdog, ē = 11.7) stay exact; p5 is used only for skips, and a lower p5 skips less (safe side). For measured rows, the calibration run reports the fitted against the measured p5. Changes section 18: CHANGES C5. |

| Q9 | 2026-10-28 | M1.5, M1.8 (built on the literal reading; confirm or correct) | 8.5: R_q = "the sum of the remaining mean time of the running tasks". Literal reading, used: max(0, Ê − elapsed) per running task. Then Ŵ is exact in the mean for c_q = 1 with deterministic run times (M/D/1), as 8.5 says. With exponential run times the expected remaining time is Ê whatever has elapsed, so the literal R underestimates and Ŵ is not exact in the mean (M/M/1). If "exact in the mean" must hold for every shape, R would be the expected remaining time given the elapsed time, which depends on the run-time shape (Q7).
