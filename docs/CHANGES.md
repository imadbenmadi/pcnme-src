| C4 | 2026-10-28 | 8.5 (and 8.3, 7.2, 11.1) | The pooled estimate Ŵ = (R + B) ÷ c treats a node's c_q slots as one server, so one long run is spread over every slot. In S1–S3 (T2 a pebble) the executor refuses T1 and T3 it could run, and the agent's Ŵ input and its "can meet the deadline" mask are off by about 2× too. | Each node publishes the remaining time r_i of each of its c_q slots instead of their sum R; Ŵ is the time W at which the backlog B, poured onto the slots in the order they free up, fills them all: Σ max(0, W − r_i) = B (0 while a slot is free).
